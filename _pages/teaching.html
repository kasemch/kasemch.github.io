--- layout: single title: "Teaching" permalink: /teaching/ author_profile: true --- My teaching focuses on health education, physical education, school health, health promotion, and the design and evaluation of learning in higher education. ## Teaching Areas - Health Education and Health Promotion - Physical Education - School Health - Sexuality Education - Drugs and Substance Abuse Education - Mental Health Promotion - Health Programme Planning and Evaluation - Curriculum and Learning Outcome Design - Assessment and Evaluation in Health and Physical Education ## Selected Courses Selected undergraduate teaching includes: - **HED2503 — Sexuality Education** - **HED3502 — Drugs and Substance Abuse** - **HED3504 — Mental Health Promotion** - **HED1101 — Health Promotion for Life** - **PED1101 — Principles and Philosophy of Physical Education** - **RHE4404 — School Health Program and Evaluation** ## Approach to Teaching My teaching practice emphasises active learning, case-based learning, structured discussion, assessment aligned with learning outcomes, and learning environments that support participation by students with diverse backgrounds and experiences. Teaching development is linked to curriculum-level work, particularly programme learning outcomes (PLOs), course learning outcomes (CLOs), curriculum mapping, assessment design, and continuous improvement. ## Teaching Development Current areas of teaching development include: - evidence-informed course and lesson design; - scenario- and case-based learning; - assessment rubrics and authentic assessment; - alignment of learning outcomes, activities, and assessment; - responsible use of AI and digital tools in higher education; - reflective teaching and professional development.
