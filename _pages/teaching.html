Evidence Governance

How public teaching status is determined

  • Central MR30 and official MR30 supplementary notices have priority for offering status.
  • Later authoritative notices supersede earlier opening or timetable evidence.
  • Teaching-load and course-quality documents provide supporting context but cannot reverse an official cancellation.
  • Restricted source documents are represented as metadata only until public sharing is explicitly approved.
  • HEPE validation and AI-assisted verification snapshots remain reconciliation-only.
  • Semester 2/2569 remains fail-closed until an authoritative assignment source is found.
Teaching information is reconciled against Ramkhamhaeng University MR30 records and traceable academic documents. Student-identifiable records, grades, answer scripts, restricted Drive identifiers, private audit metadata and confidential HEPE records are not published on this website.
