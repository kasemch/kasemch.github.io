Pedagogy

Approach to Teaching

Teaching practice emphasises active learning, case-based learning, structured discussion, assessment aligned with learning outcomes, and learning environments that support participation by students with diverse backgrounds and experiences.

Teaching development is linked to programme learning outcomes (PLOs), course learning outcomes (CLOs), curriculum mapping, assessment design, and continuous improvement.
