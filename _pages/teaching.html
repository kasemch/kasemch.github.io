Pedagogy

Approach to Teaching

Teaching practice emphasises active learning, case-based learning, structured discussion, assessment aligned with learning outcomes, and learning environments that support participation by students with diverse backgrounds and experiences.

Teaching development is linked to programme learning outcomes (PLOs), course learning outcomes (CLOs), curriculum mapping, assessment design, and continuous improvement.

Evidence Governance

How teaching status is determined

  • Central MR30 and official MR30 supplementary notices take precedence for offering status.
  • Later opening, change, instructor, and cancellation notices supersede earlier timetable evidence where they conflict.
  • Teaching-load and course-quality documents provide supporting context but do not override a later official cancellation.
  • HEPE validation datasets are reconciliation-only unless a canonical active record is independently confirmed.
Student-identifiable records, grades, answer scripts, private learning evidence, and other protected student information are not published on this website.
