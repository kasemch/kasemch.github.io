  <h2>Internal operation</h2>
  <p>This route is retained temporarily for migration compatibility. Uploading documents, classifying evidence for storage, and writing to Google Drive belong to the authenticated AWOS Internal Workspace and require explicit human authorization.</p>

  <p>The public website remains a read-only publication and discovery layer. No Google OAuth client, Drive write scope, destination folder identifiers, or upload runtime is loaded on this page.</p>

  <p>Public evidence that has passed the AWOS Publication Gate may still be viewed through the <a href="{{ '/evidence-explorer/' | relative_url }}">Evidence Explorer</a>. Return to the <a href="{{ '/' | relative_url }}">AWOS Public Hub</a>.</p>
