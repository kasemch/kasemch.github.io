## Calendar policy

This public page is designed to display only events explicitly marked for public visibility. Private teaching schedules, meetings, deadlines, and personal events must remain outside the public build unless deliberately published.

Google Calendar will become the calendar source of truth in Phase 2. Until then, the component uses demonstration/public placeholder events only.
