## Curriculum Development and Educational Quality

My academic work in curriculum and quality focuses on connecting programme intentions, course design, learning activities, assessment, evidence, and continuous improvement into a coherent system.

### Curriculum Design

Key areas include programme learning outcomes, course learning outcomes, curriculum mapping, constructive alignment, curriculum review and revision, and the design of evidence that demonstrates how learning outcomes are developed and assessed across a programme.

### Assessment and Programme Evaluation

This work includes assessment design, rubric development, course-level and programme-level evaluation, interpretation of learning evidence, and the use of evaluation findings to improve teaching and curriculum decisions.

### Quality Assurance

Quality-related work includes programme-level quality development, evidence management, internal review, continuous improvement, and the application of quality-assurance frameworks in higher education, including AUN-QA where appropriate.

### Evidence and Traceability

A recurring principle is that academic quality should be supported by clear, traceable evidence rather than duplicated documentation. Digital approaches can help connect curriculum decisions, learning outcomes, assessment evidence, review findings, and improvement actions while preserving academic accountability.
