## Academic Innovation and Selected Projects

This section presents selected academic innovation and project work that connects health and physical education, curriculum and quality, digital technology, artificial intelligence, and community-oriented academic service.

### AI & Digital Innovation

Areas of interest include responsible AI-supported academic workflows, digital curriculum governance, evidence management, teaching support, assessment support, and quality-assurance systems designed to reduce duplicated work while maintaining traceability and academic oversight.

### Educational Systems and Prototypes

Selected prototype work explores how digital systems can support programme management, curriculum alignment, evidence tracking, academic review, and decision-making. Public descriptions focus on educational purpose, design principles, and lessons learned rather than restricted technical details.

### Health and Community Service Innovation

Project interests include health screening, physical fitness, wellbeing, health promotion, participant-facing health information, and community service models that connect university expertise with practical public benefit.

### Public Scholarship

This area includes academic communication, educational resources, and selected work that connects health, physical education, history, culture, and public understanding.

## Disclosure Principle

Only information appropriate for public release is published here. Internal architecture, credentials, restricted data, non-production implementation details, confidential governance materials, and other protected project information are intentionally excluded.
