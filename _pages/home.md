## Health & Physical Education · Curriculum & Quality · Academic Innovation

Welcome to my academic website. My work focuses on health and physical education, curriculum development, learning and assessment, educational quality assurance, and the thoughtful use of digital technology and artificial intelligence in higher education.

### Academic Focus

- **Health & Physical Education** — teaching, learning, assessment, school health, health promotion, and physical education.
- **Curriculum & Quality** — outcome-based curriculum design, programme learning outcomes, course alignment, curriculum mapping, programme evaluation, and educational quality assurance.
- **Research** — health and movement behaviours, physical activity, educational innovation, and applied research in health and physical education.
- **AI & Digital Innovation** — responsible use of AI and digital systems to support teaching, academic work, curriculum governance, evidence management, and educational services.
- **Academic Service & Community** — applying academic knowledge to health promotion, professional development, and community-oriented educational initiatives.
