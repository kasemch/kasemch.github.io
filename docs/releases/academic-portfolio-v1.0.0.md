## Visual baseline
- Academic Portfolio Studio language: navy / brick / cream / white.
- Georgia/serif academic headings with system-sans body copy.
- Evidence cards, lifecycle views, descriptive dashboards, narrative storytelling, status badges, public-safe boundaries and responsive layouts.
- Shared token foundation in `assets/css/academic-portfolio-tokens.css`.

## Evidence baseline
- Teaching offering status remains governed by authoritative MR30 reconciliation.
- Research project status remains governed by the public research registry and evidence matrix.
- Publication categories remain separated into journal publications, conference proceedings and degree research.
- Publication-project relationships are displayed only when explicitly verified.
- Restricted/private evidence remains excluded or metadata-only.
- Descriptive analytics are not converted into performance rankings or unsupported completion percentages.
