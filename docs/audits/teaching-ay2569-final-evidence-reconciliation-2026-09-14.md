## Decision framework

The public Teaching profile follows an evidence-first, latest-authority-wins model. Central MR30 and official MR30 supplementary notices determine offering status. Controlled teaching-load records and course-quality documents may support instructor, timetable, or quality-document provenance, but they cannot override a later authoritative cancellation notice. HEPE validation/inactive records are reconciliation-only.

## Source hierarchy used

1. Central MR30 / official MR30 supplementary notices.
2. Official university/faculty/department records.
3. Reconciled teaching-load and timetable evidence.
4. TQF3, TQF5, verification and course-improvement materials.
5. HEPE canonical/reconciliation data only where independently supported; SOURCEB-VALIDATION and inactive records are excluded from public inference.
