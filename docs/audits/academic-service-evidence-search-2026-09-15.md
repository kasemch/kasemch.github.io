# Academic Service & Community Engagement Evidence Search — 2026-09-15

Status: NEEDS-EVIDENCE / FAIL-CLOSED

## Purpose
Assess whether the public academic website can safely promote a dedicated Academic Service & Community Engagement section using existing repository and Google Drive evidence.

## Search scope
Targeted Drive searches were conducted using Thai and English-adjacent terms covering academic service, community engagement, health, physical education, social service, and Ramkhamhaeng University context.

## Evidence found
Searches surfaced materials primarily in these categories:
- programme SAR / AUN-QA reports;
- curriculum and instructor databases;
- course and TQF documents;
- research reports;
- programme meeting and operational records.

These records may contain references to outreach or service activities, but they are not, by themselves, authoritative public evidence of a specific academic-service project, named community engagement activity, beneficiary group, or verified outcome attributable to the academic profile.

## Decision
Do not create a dedicated named-project Academic Service page or add named beneficiaries/outcomes at this time.

Retain only the existing high-level public-safe description of academic service/community engagement until a project-level evidence package is available.

## Promotion gate
A future item may be promoted only after all of the following are verified:
1. project/activity identity and date;
2. the user's documented role or contribution;
3. source authority and recency;
4. public-sharing permission / confidentiality review;
5. no student, participant, beneficiary, or restricted institutional data exposure;
6. outcome wording supported by evidence rather than inference.

## Public rule
Do not infer "academic service" from curriculum, teaching, research, SAR, or meeting records merely because community or outreach language appears in those records.
