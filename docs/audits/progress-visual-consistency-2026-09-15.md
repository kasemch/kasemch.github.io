# Progress Visualization Consistency Audit

Date: 2026-09-15
Scope: Research Milestone Map, Academic Textbook Writing Book Journey, Curriculum & Academic Quality Improvement Cycle.

## Shared visual grammar
- Verified: green (`--pv-verified`)
- Active/current: blue (`--pv-active`)
- Next gate: gold (`--pv-next`)
- Future/unconfirmed: muted grey (`--pv-future`)
- Locked: darker neutral grey (`--pv-locked`)
- Standard progress node: 50px
- Shared focus treatment for scrollable/interactive progress visuals
- Shared reduced-motion handling

## Pattern-specific meaning preserved
- Research remains a milestone map based on registry and evidence-matrix status.
- Academic Writing remains a gated Book Journey and does not use invented percentages.
- Curriculum & QA remains an explanatory improvement cycle rather than a project-progress tracker.
- Teaching remains an evidence dashboard because semester/course status is not one sequential lifecycle.
- Professional Development remains a credential registry because credentials are not a mandatory ordered journey.

## Public-safety finding
No factual, evidence-status, manuscript, teaching-status, credential, or curriculum-governance data is changed by this harmonization. The change is presentation-only, plus keyboard focusability for the curriculum cycle visual.
