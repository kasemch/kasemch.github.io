## 7. Admission rule for future promotion

A TQF4/TQF5/TQF6 template may be considered for promotion beyond UNDER_REVIEW only when at least one of the following is available and verified:

1. University regulation, announcement, order, or circular explicitly defining the template.
2. Official blank form hosted by an authoritative Ramkhamhaeng University unit.
3. Controlled document from the university/faculty QA or academic-affairs authority with clear template ownership.
4. Signed/dated approval minutes or order adopting the specific HEPE template as the controlled programme/faculty template.
5. Verified institutional document registry metadata that binds the template ID/version to an authorized owner and effective date.

A mere occurrence in a course report, programme report, Smart QMS document, or user-created Drive file is not enough.
